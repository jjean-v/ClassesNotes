#### Weather vs Climate


**Weather**: State of the atmosphere in a given place and time. e.g. "Today is a hot day in London", "Today is a cold day in Nairobi“…

**Climate**: Climate is the Average Weather, made by many meteorological events, on a large region. Climate warming is a global phenomenon

* Global Temperature difference (since pre-industrial era)
* Global Land-Ocean Temperature Index

### Challenges: Adaptation and Mitigation

**ADAPTATION**

* Even if policies and efforts to reduce emissions prove effective, some climate change is inevitable
* Strategies and actions to adapt to its impacts are also needed
* Anticipate the adverse effects of climate change
* Take appropriate action to prevent or minimize the damages or take advantage of potential opportunities
* Plan early adaptation action to save lives and money later
* Change living place due to hot weather, water scarcity or sea level increase

**MITIGATION**

* In order to stabilize GHGs concentrations in the atmosphere, emissions would need to peak and decline thereafter.
* Mitigation efforts over the next decade will have a large impact on opportunities to achieve lower stabilization levels
* Mitigation is needed to stabilize rising temperatures, avoid irreversible and catastrophic changes
* Big transformation of the energy-system, a fast scale-up of mitigation action in all sectors, leading to emission reductions as soon as possible
* Mitigate the hot weather in urban area => Vegetalize the cities

# Nuclear Technology

## Introduction

### Nuclear Fission

From radioactivity to transmutation:

* 1895. Ionising radiation, X-rays(W. Rontgen)
* 1896. Radioactivity of uranium (H. Becquerel)
* 1899. Alpha and beta decay (E. Rutherford), Gamma decay (P. Villard)
* 1917. Nuclear transmutation, 14N + α17O + p (E. Rutherford)

From transmutation to nuclear fission:

* 1932. Discovery of the neutron (J. Chadwick)
* 1934. Induced radioactivity (I. Joliot-Curie, F.Joliot-Curie)
* 1934. Neutron thermalization (E. Fermi & via Panisperna boys)

**Main challenge when starting a nuclear fission is to moderate it and controle it.**

From nuclear fission to nuclear energy:

* 1942. Controlled nuclear chain reaction. In CP-1(Chicago Pile No.1) a controlled fission chain reaction of uranium-235 nuclei based on slowed-down (thermal) neutrons was obtained for 28 minutes
